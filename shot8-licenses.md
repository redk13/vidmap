## Animation scope

A silent 10-second Remotion composition. The reference-guided book is cropped to a close 16:9 framing with only a 1.5% push-in. Surroundings dim, then a strong compact spotlight isolates the exact target line. The transparent yellow-and-ink content is revealed left-to-right in frames 72–104 after the light and remains visible. No tearing, floating cards, platform logos, camera shake or production-video integration.

The user explicitly permits choosing different verified data or different data-graphic elements for each future production. The values in this sample are not mandatory fixed content for later uses.
