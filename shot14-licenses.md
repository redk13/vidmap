# Shot Lab 14 — data, sources and licenses

## Verified data used in the sample
- Article: **How many of Australia’s 2.2 million property investors would lose out under a new plan to curb negative gearing?**
- Publisher: **The Conversation**, published **August 5, 2025**.
- URL: https://theconversation.com/how-many-of-australias-2-2-million-property-investors-would-lose-out-under-a-new-plan-to-curb-negative-gearing-262595
- The article cites RMIT researcher Liam Davies’ analysis of **ATO Taxation Statistics 2022-23, Table 27**.
- ATO data resource: https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/taxation-statistics-2022-23/resource/bf075e6e-3ee2-4472-8764-7d65df285bc5

### Figures and rounding
- Total individual investors with an interest in rental property: **2,261,080** → displayed as **2.26 million**.
- Negatively geared investors: **1,117,175 (49.4%)** → displayed as **1.12M**.
- Neutral/profit remainder calculated from the published total: 2,261,080 − 1,117,175 = **1,143,905** → displayed as **1.14M**.
- Net rental losses/deductions claimed by negatively geared investors in 2022-23: **A$10.4 billion** → displayed as **Over $10 billion**.
- These are historical 2022-23 figures, not a forecast or a 2026 estimate.
