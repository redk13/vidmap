- Text: “Water becomes vapor”, “Clouds form”, “Rain, snow or hail”, and “Rivers & oceans”.
- Factual reference: **USGS Water Science School — Water Cycle**.
- URL: https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/water-cycle
- USGS describes evaporation of liquid water into vapor, condensation forming clouds, precipitation returning water to the surface, and oceans/rivers as stores and pathways in the continuing cycle. No forecast, numerical claim or unsupported causal statistic is used.
